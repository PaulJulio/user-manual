# Personal User Manual: Research on Sections & Questions

This document compiles common sections and questions found in "Personal User Manuals" (also known as "Working with Me" guides).

## 1. Introduction & The Basics
**Goal:** Provide a high-level overview of who you are and your role.
- How do you pronounce your name? What are your pronouns?
- What is your official role and your actual "day-to-day" scope?
- What should people come to you for?
- What are your core values as a professional?

## 2. Communication Preferences
**Goal:** Define how you like to exchange information to reduce friction.
- What is your preferred channel for:
    - Quick questions? (e.g., Slack/Teams)
    - Complex topics or long-form thoughts? (e.g., Email, Doc)
    - Urgent "fires"? (e.g., Phone call, direct ping)
- What is your typical response time for different channels?
- Do you prefer asynchronous updates or synchronous meetings?
- How do you feel about "brain-dump" messages vs. polished updates?

## 3. Ideal Working Conditions & Calendar
**Goal:** Help others understand your rhythm and peak productivity times.
- What are your core working hours?
- When is your "Deep Work" time? (When should meetings be avoided?)
- How do you manage your calendar? (e.g., "If it's not on the calendar, it doesn't exist.")
- What environment helps you thrive? (e.g., Quiet for focus, collaborative for brainstorming.)
- How do you handle time zone differences (if applicable)?

## 4. Feedback & Recognition
**Goal:** Ensure growth and appreciation are handled in a way that resonates with you.
- How do you like to *receive* feedback? (e.g., Real-time, in writing beforehand, during 1:1s, direct vs. softened.)
- How do you like to *give* feedback?
- How do you like to be recognized for a job well done? (e.g., Public shout-out, private note, small gift/token.)
- What is the best way to course-correct with you if something isn't working?

## 5. Strengths, Blind Spots & Quirks
**Goal:** Manage expectations and leverage your unique working style.
- What are your "superpowers"? (What do you love doing and do well?)
- What are your "blind spots" or areas where you struggle?
- What are your "quirks"? (e.g., "I think out loud," "I might look grumpy when I'm actually just concentrating," "I am a perfectionist about X.")
- What should people *not* expect from you?

## 6. Troubleshooting: Stress & Conflict
**Goal:** Provide a "manual" for when things aren't going perfectly.
- How can people tell when you are stressed or overwhelmed? (e.g., "I get quiet," "I start micromanaging.")
- What is the best way to support you when you're under pressure?
- What are your "pet peeves" or triggers in a work environment?
- How do you prefer to resolve disagreements or conflict?

## 7. Personal Interests (Optional)
**Goal:** Build rapport and human connection.
- What do you do for fun outside of work?
- What are you currently learning or curious about?
- Are there any personal boundaries you'd like to share? (e.g., "I don't check messages after 6 PM because of family time.")
