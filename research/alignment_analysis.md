# Alignment Analysis: Values vs. Stakeholder Scope

This document analyzes the relationship between core professional values and the defined scope for stakeholders to ensure architectural consistency in the personal user manual.

## Mapping Table

| Value | Stakeholder Scope | Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trust & Radical Accountability** | **High-Signal Synthesis** | **High Alignment.** Reliability is the foundation of a high-fidelity interface. Providing "Early Warnings" requires the stakeholder to trust the messenger implicitly. |
| **Relentless Excellence** | **Strategic Alignment & Priority Negotiation** | **High Alignment.** "Risk:reward" and "cost of change" assessments are calibrated against world-class performance standards, ensuring negotiation isn't just about speed, but quality. |
| **Mission & Value Integrity** | **Cross-Functional Unblocking & Tie-Breaking** | **Direct Link.** Tie-breaking is defined as "mission-aligned." The value provides the decision-making framework for the action. |

## Strategic Considerations

- **Speed vs. Perfection:** To prevent the "Excellence" value from delaying the "High-Signal" scope, the focus remains on expert **judgment** and **assessment** rather than just raw data.
- **Accountability vs. Transparency:** "Radical Accountability" reinforces the "Signal" by ensuring that "Early Warnings" and "Blockers" are reported honestly to advance the mission, rather than over-filtering to protect optics.
