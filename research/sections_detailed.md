# Personal User Manual: Comprehensive Research & Granular Questions

This document is a "master list" of questions and sub-questions for building a personal user manual. It errs on the side of extreme detail, allowing you to pick and choose what resonates.

---

## 1. Introduction & Professional Identity
**Core Goal:** Set the stage for who you are and what you stand for.

*   **Who are you?**
    *   What is your preferred name/pronouns?
    *   What is your "elevator pitch" for your role?
    *   What are your top 3 professional values (e.g., Transparency, Craft, Speed, Empathy)?
*   **What is your scope?**
    *   What are the top 3 things people *should* come to you for?
    *   What are the things people *should not* come to you for (or that you are trying to delegate)?
    *   What are you currently learning or trying to get better at?

## 2. Communication Style & Preferences
**Core Goal:** Minimize the "guessing game" of daily interaction.

*   **Channels & Response Times:**
    *   What is your "Default" channel for general chat?
    *   What is the best way to get a *fast* answer (under 10 mins)?
    *   How should someone handle "Urgent/Fire" situations?
    *   What is your expected turnaround for:
        *   Slack/Teams messages?
        *   Emails?
        *   PR Reviews (if applicable)?
        *   Document comments?
*   **Synchronous vs. Asynchronous:**
    *   Do you prefer "thinking out loud" in a meeting or "writing it out" in a doc?
    *   How do you feel about unscheduled "pings" or "drive-by" calls?
    *   Do you prefer "Brain-dump" messages (fragmented thoughts) or "Polished" updates?
*   **Nuance & Tone:**
    *   How should people interpret your silence in a meeting? (Processing? Disagreeing? Busy?)
    *   Do you use emojis to convey tone, or are you strictly "text-only"?
    *   When you say "I'm fine with that," does it mean "I love it" or "I don't have the energy to argue"?

## 3. Feedback & Recognition
**Core Goal:** Optimize how you grow and how you are appreciated.

*   **Receiving Feedback:**
    *   **Timing:** Do you want "Hot Feedback" (immediate) or "Cold Feedback" (during a 1:1)?
    *   **Format:** Do you want it in writing *before* a meeting so you can process it?
    *   **Directness:** Do you prefer "Radical Candor" (blunt) or a "Feedback Sandwich" (softened)?
    *   **Trigger Words:** Are there certain words or phrases that make you defensive?
*   **Giving Feedback:**
    *   How can others expect you to deliver feedback to them?
    *   What is the best way for someone to "course-correct" you if you're off track?
*   **Recognition:**
    *   Do you prefer public shout-outs (Slack #general, Standup) or private notes?
    *   What kind of recognition feels most meaningful to you (e.g., a "thank you," a small gift, a high-impact project)?

## 4. Ideal Working Conditions & Rhythm
**Core Goal:** Protect your productivity and energy.

*   **The Clock:**
    *   What are your "Hard Start" and "Hard Stop" times?
    *   Are you an **Integrator** (work flows into life) or a **Segmentor** (strict boundaries)?
    *   What time of day is your "Peak Intelligence" (when you do your best thinking)?
*   **Deep Work & Flow:**
    *   What are your "No Meeting" zones?
    *   How do you signal that you are in a "Flow State" (e.g., a specific emoji, "Busy" status)?
    *   What is the "one thing" that instantly breaks your concentration?
*   **Environment:**
    *   Do you thrive in a "buzzing" environment or total silence?
    *   What tools or setups are essential for your success (e.g., dual monitors, specific IDE, "I need my camera off")?

## 5. Decision-Making & Problem Solving
**Core Goal:** Align on how choices are made to avoid frustration.

*   **Decision Style:**
    *   Are you **Analytical** (data-first), **Intuitive** (gut-first), or **Collaborative** (consensus-first)?
    *   How much "detail" do you want to see before making a decision? (High-level summary vs. deep-dive data).
    *   What decisions do you want to own, and which do you want to delegate?
*   **Technical/Professional Disagreements:**
    *   What is your "Tie-breaker" protocol when a team is at a stalemate?
    *   Do you prefer to "Whiteboard it out" or "Write an ADR/Doc"?
    *   What is the fastest way to change your mind (e.g., data, a working demo, a clean abstraction)?

## 6. Strengths, Blind Spots & Quirks
**Core Goal:** Manage expectations and humanize your working relationship.

*   **The Good & The Bad:**
    *   What are your "Superpowers" (the things you do effortlessly and love)?
    *   What are your "Blind Spots" (the things you consistently miss or struggle with)?
    *   What is something people often get *wrong* about you when they first work with you?
*   **Quirks & Habits:**
    *   "I think out loud—don't take every thought as a directive."
    *   "I am a perfectionist about [X] but messy about [Y]."
    *   "I need to 'sleep on it' before committing to big changes."
    *   What are your "Pet Peeves" or professional "Triggers"?

## 7. Troubleshooting: Stress & Conflict
**Core Goal:** A manual for when things aren't going well.

*   **Stress Signals:**
    *   How will I know when you are overwhelmed? (e.g., "I stop using emojis," "I get quiet," "I start micromanaging").
    *   What is the best way to support you when you're under pressure?
*   **Conflict Resolution:**
    *   How do you prefer to handle disagreements (e.g., direct confrontation, a mediator, a "cooling off" period)?
    *   What makes you lose trust in a colleague?
    *   What is the fastest way to *re-earn* your trust?

## 8. Personal Context: Roots, Family & Passions
**Core Goal:** Build deep rapport and explain how your background shapes your professional "Operating System."

*   **Military Background & Values:**
    *   **The "Commander's Intent":** How do you translate the concept of "Mission First" to your team? (e.g., "I need to know the *why* and the ultimate goal so I can execute autonomously.")
    *   **Communication Style:** How has military service influenced your directness? (e.g., "I value high-signal, 'no-BS' communication. If I'm too brief, it's about efficiency, not rudeness.")
    *   **Leadership & Accountability:** What does "Servant Leadership" mean to you in a civilian context? How do you handle "Chain of Command" vs. "Flat Hierarchy"?
    *   **Resilience & Crisis:** How do you react when a "plan doesn't survive first contact with the enemy"? (e.g., "I don't panic; I re-calibrate and pivot.")
    *   **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Do you have a preference for structured processes? Where do you value "Discipline" in your work?

*   **Large Family Dynamics:**
    *   **Collaboration & Community:** How does being part of a large family influence your view of "Team"? (e.g., "I naturally think 'we' before 'I'.")
    *   **Negotiation & Diplomacy:** Did you have to be a "Consensus Builder" or a "Mediator" growing up? How does that show up in meetings today?
    *   **Resourcefulness:** Growing up in a large group often requires "making it work" with what you have. How does that translate to your professional problem-solving?
    *   **Autonomy:** Did you have to "carve out your own lane"? How do you feel about being left to own a project vs. constant check-ins?

*   **Life on the Water (Boating):**
    *   **Preparation & Safety:** Does your "Captain" mentality influence your project planning? (e.g., "I check the weather and the gear twice. I am a 'measure twice, cut once' person.")
    *   **Situational Awareness:** How do you apply the "scan the horizon" skill to spotting professional risks or opportunities?
    *   **The "Unplugged" Captain:** Is the boat your place for "Deep Reflection" or "Total Disconnect"? How can colleagues respect those boundaries?
    *   **Rapport Building:** What is your favorite "Sea Story" or recent trip that people can ask you about to break the ice?
    *   **The "Anchor" Analogy:** What are the things that keep you grounded when the professional "seas" get rough?

*   **General Context & Boundaries:**
    *   What are your "Hard Stop" times for family commitments?
    *   What are you currently curious about outside of your field?
    *   What is the "one thing" from your personal life that you are most proud of?
