*Core Goal: Identify the most effective ways for us to exchange high-fidelity feedback and celebrate mission success together.*

### Receiving Feedback
- **Timing:**
  - I prefer **"Hot Feedback"** delivered in the moment or directly after a meeting or event. This allows us to course-correct in real-time while the context is fresh.
- **Format:**
  - I have **no strong preference** for the format. Whether you deliver it in writing beforehand or live in the moment, please choose the method that makes *you* most comfortable to deliver it effectively.
- **Directness:**
  - I value **Radical Candor**. While we should never be rude, I find "compliment sandwiches" confusing and they often obscure your true intent. Please be direct with your concerns. **Note:** Do not mistake my passion for the mission as stubbornness; I am always open to feedback that helps us improve.
- **Trigger Words:**
  - None at the moment, but I will update this as I encounter any. I encourage you to use the phrase **"Can I give you some feedback on X?"** to signal that you have a concern you'd like to share.

### Giving Feedback
- **How can others expect you to deliver feedback to them?**
  - By default, I will send **Slack DMs in the moment** to provide real-time feedback, followed by a deeper dive in our scheduled 1:1s. However, I am happy to adapt this to your personal preference. If you prefer a different cadence or format, let's discuss it.
- **What is the best way for someone to "course-correct" you if you're off track?**
  - Please send me a **Slack DM**. If that doesn't work or the situation is urgent, please **speak up live!** I value the mission over my ego and appreciate being held accountable.

### Recognition
- **Do you prefer public shout-outs or private notes?**
  - I am not historically used to formal recognition and may not always appear to "accept" it well, this is something I am actively working on! Don't let my reaction discourage you from sharing wins.
- **What kind of recognition feels most meaningful to you?**
  - I feel most appreciated when I see the **combined efforts of our team recognized in broad communications**. Highlighting the team's impact on the mission is the ultimate "thank you" for me.
